3.1: From AI Prototype To Production: What Changes?

Engineering AI systems that work in the real world.

Building an AI prototype is getting easier.

A few prompts.

A model.

Some data.

A working demo.

It can look impressive very quickly.

But production is a different story.

Once AI becomes part of a real enterprise platform, the questions change.

  • How is it secured?
  • How is it evaluated?
  • How do we observe it?
  • What happens when it fails?
  • What does it cost to operate?
  • Who owns the decisions it makes?

A prototype is designed to prove that something is possible.

A production system needs to prove that it can be trusted.

This is where architecture becomes important.

The model is only one part of the system.

Around it, we need:

  • Reliable data
  • Identity and security
  • Governance
  • Observability
  • Evaluation
  • Resilience
  • Human oversight

The biggest shift is simple:

From “Can we make AI work?”

To “Can we operate it safely and reliably?”

That is the difference between an AI experiment and an enterprise AI capability.
