JCX Series 2 — Advanced Enterprise Architecture & AI Systems

Series 2 focuses on the next stage of modern platforms — operating intelligent, distributed systems at scale. Moving beyond cloud foundations, this series explores how AI, event-driven design, and platform engineering come together under real enterprise constraints.

In Series 1, the focus was on building strong foundations — Landing Zones, Responsible AI, Observability, DevSecOps, and delivery discipline.

Series 2 moves into a different class of challenges:

  • How systems evolve over time
  • How they behave under scale and pressure
  • How trust is maintained in automated decision-making
  • How architecture adapts to complexity, regulation, and cost constraints

This is where architecture shifts from designing components to operating systems.

Event-Driven AI Systems

Designing AI as part of distributed, real-time systems driven by events and state changes.

Multi-Agent Architectures

Structuring AI agents with clear roles, boundaries, and coordination models for predictable behaviour.

AI Platform Engineering

Building scalable platforms with shared services, governance, and operating models that support AI at scale.

Observability & Trust

Tracing decisions, monitoring behaviour, and ensuring transparency in AI-driven systems.

Operating AI at Scale

Managing cost, governance, resilience, and organisational alignment in enterprise AI platforms.

Architecture as Responsibility

Designing systems that are secure, scalable, and sustainable in real-world enterprise environments.
