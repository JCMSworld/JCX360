From Engineering To Technology Leadership

Looking across these projects, the progression is not simply from one job title to another.

It is a progression in the scale and nature of the problems being solved.

The early work was deeply technical — designing applications, writing code, working with databases, building integrations and understanding how systems operate at a detailed level.

That engineering foundation developed into technical leadership, solution architecture, cloud transformation, programme delivery and eventually enterprise-scale technology and AI delivery.

Code → Systems → Architecture → Engineering Leadership → Delivery Leadership → Enterprise Transformation → AI
The technology changed significantly over the years. The underlying focus remained the same: understanding the problem, designing the right solution and making sure it can actually be delivered.
